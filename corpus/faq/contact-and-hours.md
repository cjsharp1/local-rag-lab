# Contact and Hours

Paddle Section Supply is a canoe and kayak outfitter in Ashby Landing, on the shore of Ashby Lake. Our dock sits on Ashby Lake, at the edge of the Kettle Basin Canoe Area.

Phone is the fastest way to reach us during store hours. Email is best for reservation changes, questions about routes and anything you want in writing. Please include your name and trip dates in every email so we can find your booking quickly.

## Season and store hours

Our 2026 season runs from **May 15 to October 10**.

During the season, the store is open **6 a.m. to 8 p.m., every day**, including weekends and holidays. The early opening is on purpose: many groups want to be on the water at first light, and we would rather be here when you are.

Rental gear can be picked up from 7 a.m. on the first day of your rental. Overnight permits can be picked up at the store up to 24 hours before your entry.

## Returning gear after hours

If you come off the water after the store closes, use the **after-hours return drop** next to the store. It has a boat rack for canoes and kayaks and a key box for the rest.

- The after-hours drop is open until **10 p.m.**
- Gear is due back by **6 p.m.** on the last day of your rental. Gear dropped after 6 p.m. still counts as late, even though the drop is open later.
- Put boats on the rack hull-up, and leave paddles, PFDs and smaller gear beside them under the rack's cover.
- Put any keys or tags in the key box with a note listing your name and the gear you returned.

We check in after-hours returns when the store opens the next morning. If something is damaged or missing, staff will contact you.

## Calling from the field

There is no cell service past Ashby Lake. If you are running late or need to change your exit plans, call us as soon as you are back in range, or send a message from a satellite messenger if you carry one. The rental agreement explains how late returns are handled.

## Getting here

Ashby Landing sits at the end of the road on Ashby Lake. Follow the signs for the public landing; the store is on Dock Road, just up from the water. Park in the outfitter lot beside the store; parking rates are on the current rate sheet.
