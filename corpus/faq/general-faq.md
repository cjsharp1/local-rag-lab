# Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most at the counter and on the phone. If your question is about a specific policy, the policy documents linked from our website have the full details and the exact amounts.

## Planning and arriving

### Can I bring my dog?

Yes. Dogs are welcome on trips. Keep your dog leashed at the landing and on our dock, where there are boats being loaded, other paddlers and other dogs. Out on the trip, keep your dog under control at campsites and on portages, and remember that a nervous dog in a canoe shifts the boat; a short practice paddle near shore before you leave helps.

### Will my phone work out there?

Not for long. There is no cell service past Ashby Lake. Once you leave Ashby Lake, assume you cannot call, text or load a map. Download maps before you leave, and tell someone at home your route and expected exit date. If you want a way to call for help, rent a satellite messenger with your gear.

### Where do I park, and what does it cost?

Park in the outfitter lot next to the store. Parking is **$8 per day**. Pay at the counter when you pick up your gear, and leave the parking tag on your dashboard.

### Are there showers?

Yes. Hot showers are available at the store for a **$5 token**, sold at the counter. Most people buy one on the way out of the woods.

### Do you offer a group discount?

Yes. Groups that rent **3 or more boats for 5 or more days** get **10% off boat rentals**. The discount applies to the boats, not to camping gear or other items.

### Do I need a permit?

Almost certainly. Overnight trips during the permit quota season need an overnight permit for a specific entry point and entry date, and day trips need a permit too. We can issue overnight permits at the store. See the permits and regulations page for fees, pickup rules and the regulations every group must follow.

### When are you open?

We are open daily during the season. See the contact and hours page for exact hours and the after-hours return drop.

## Money and policies

### What happens if we come back late?

Late returns are charged per boat, per day. The rental agreement has the details, including what to do if weather keeps you off the water on your last day.

### How does the deposit work?

We take a deposit for each boat as a hold on your credit card when you pick up, and release it after you return the gear in good shape. Gear-only rentals have a smaller deposit, and you can leave a cash deposit instead of a card hold. The deposits and payment policy lists the amounts and how long the release takes.

### What if we damage a canoe?

Normal scratches and scuffs are part of canoeing and cost you nothing. Real damage, like a cracked hull or a broken yoke, is charged according to our damage and loss policy. You can buy a damage waiver at pickup that limits what you pay for damage to a boat. Read the damage and loss policy for the charges and what the waiver does and does not cover.

### What if I need to cancel?

How much you get back depends on how far ahead you cancel. The cancellation and weather policy explains the refund and credit rules, and what happens if the Wilderness Office closes the area before your trip.

### Can I take rented gear somewhere else?

No. Rented gear stays in the Kettle Basin Canoe Area, and it may not be towed behind a motorboat. The rental agreement has the full rule.

## Boats and gear

### Which canoe should I rent?

For most pairs, the Northline 17 Kevlar. It is light enough to carry comfortably on portages and it is our most-rented boat. If you are not planning to portage much and want something you do not have to baby, the Northline 17 Aluminum is heavier but nearly indestructible. The boats and gear catalog lists every boat with its weight and capacity.

### How do I keep from damaging the canoe?

Carry it; do not slide it. Whenever the hull would touch rock, gravel or the landing, pick the boat up and set it down, even if you only need to move it a few feet. Dragging a canoe grinds through the hull faster than anything else you can do to it. Load and unload with the boat floating, not beached. Our canoe care guide has the full routine.

### Do you rent kayaks?

Yes. We rent the Driftline 16, a single touring kayak with a rudder and two hatches, and the Driftline 21, a tandem touring kayak with a rudder. Kayaks suit routes with big open water and few portages better than routes with long carries. Ask us which routes fit.

### What forms of payment do you take?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, Discover and cash. We do not accept personal checks. The deposits and payment policy explains when each payment is due.

### Is a paddle and life jacket included?

Yes. Every boat rental includes paddles, a PFD for each person, yoke pads and a 50-ft rope. See the PFD sizing guide to make sure everyone gets one that fits.

### Can you shuttle us to a far entry point?

Yes. We run a tow boat and a van to several entry points. Reserve ahead; the shuttle service policy has departure times and booking rules, and prices are on the current rate sheet.

### Do you sell stove fuel?

Yes. We sell isobutane canisters at the counter. See the stove and fuel guide for sizes and how to carry fuel on our shuttles.

## Still have a question?

Call or email us. Our contact details are on the contact and hours page. We would rather answer a question before your trip than solve a problem during it.
