# Boats and Gear Catalog

This catalog covers every boat, tent, pack, and stove we rent at Paddle Section Supply for trips into the Kettle Basin Canoe Area. It lists weights, capacities, and what each piece is good at, so you can match the gear to your route before you call the desk. For daily and weekly prices, see the current rate sheet. Questions about a specific piece of gear go to desk@paddlesectionsupply.example or (555) 555-0147.

## What comes with every boat

Every boat rental includes:

- **Paddles:** 2 per canoe, 1 per kayak seat
- **One PFD per person** in your group (see our PFD sizing guide for fit)
- **Yoke pads** for portaging
- **A 50-ft rope** for lining, tying off at camp, or hanging a food pack

You don't need to ask for these. They go into the boat at pickup.

## Canoes

### Northline 17 Kevlar

The Northline 17 Kevlar is a 17 ft tandem canoe that weighs 44 lb and carries up to 900 lb. It is the most-rented boat in our fleet, and for good reason: it is light enough for one person to portage on the yoke, roomy enough for two paddlers and a week of gear, and steady in a chop. If you are planning a route with long carries, such as anything with portages over 100 rods, this is the boat we'll steer you toward first.

### Northline 17 Aluminum

The Northline 17 Aluminum shares the 17 ft tandem layout but weighs 72 lb and carries up to 850 lb. It is nearly indestructible, which makes it the right pick for rocky shorelines, shallow beaver-dam creeks, and first trips where the boat is going to get bumped. The trade-offs are weight on the portage trail and noise: aluminum rings when a paddle knocks the gunwale.

### Wayfarer 18.5

The Wayfarer 18.5 is an 18.5 ft three-person Kevlar canoe with three seats. It weighs 52 lb and carries up to 1,150 lb. It suits a family with one child or three adults who want to stay in one boat. It is long, so it tracks well on open lakes but takes more care turning on narrow creeks.

### Kestrel 15

The Kestrel 15 is a 15 ft solo Kevlar canoe that weighs 32 lb and carries up to 425 lb. It is the lightest boat we rent and an easy carry for a solo traveler. The center seat lets one paddler trim the boat level.

### A note on Kevlar hulls

Kevlar is what makes our lightest canoes light, but it is brittle when it meets rock. A Kevlar hull shrugs off long days of paddling, yet a hard hit on a submerged boulder or a drop onto a rocky landing can crack or puncture the layup where an aluminum hull would only dent. In a Kevlar boat, slow down in shallow water, step out before the hull touches bottom, and carry it over rough ground rather than sliding it. Our canoe care guide goes into more detail.

## Kayaks

### Driftline 16

The Driftline 16 is a single touring kayak that weighs 54 lb. It has a rudder and two hatches, one fore and one aft, for gear. It is a good choice for big-water routes where wind is the main hazard, such as the Raven Narrows Big Water route.

### Driftline 21

The Driftline 21 is a tandem touring kayak that weighs 78 lb and has a rudder. It is fast and stable for two paddlers, but at 78 lb it is a two-person carry on every portage. Pick it for routes with few, short portages.

Kayak gear has to fit through hatches, so pack in smaller dry bags rather than one large portage pack.

## Tents

### Ridgeback 2

The Ridgeback 2 is a 2-person tent that weighs 4.8 lb. It packs small enough to ride inside a Portage Pro 60 with room left for sleeping bags.

### Ridgeback 4

The Ridgeback 4 is a 4-person tent that weighs 8.6 lb. Two people can share it with room to spread out gear on rainy days, or four can sleep in it shoulder to shoulder. Split the poles and the body between two packs to balance the load.

Both tents come with a rainfly, stakes, and a ground cloth in a single stuff sack.

## Packs

### Portage Pro 60

The Portage Pro 60 is a 60-liter portage pack. It is the right size for a solo paddler's whole kit or for one person's share of a tandem trip. It stands upright in the canoe and carries comfortably on short portages.

### Portage Pro 80

The Portage Pro 80 is an 80-liter portage pack, sized for the tent, sleeping bags, and pads for two people. Loaded, it sits low in the center of the canoe and keeps the boat stable.

Both packs have shoulder straps, a hip belt, and a tumpline. Line them with a heavy plastic pack liner (we include one) because canoe packs are not waterproof on their own.

## Food storage

- **Food barrel (60 L, with harness):** a hard-sided barrel that rides in the canoe and carries like a pack on portages. It keeps food dry and keeps animals and rodents out.
- **Bear-resistant canister:** a smaller hard container that meets the Wilderness Office's food storage rule. Bear-resistant containers are required on Heron Lake, Gull Lake, and Cutfoot Lake, so plan on at least one canister for any route that camps on those lakes.

Elsewhere you may hang food 12 ft up and 6 ft out from the trunk instead, which is what the 50-ft rope that comes with your boat is for.

## Stove

### StormCell 2

The StormCell 2 is a canister stove that burns isobutane, boils 1 liter of water in about 4 minutes, and has an on/off valve. That valve matters: under Stage 2 fire restrictions, campfires are banned and only stoves with an on/off valve may be used. Canisters are sold at the store. See our stove and fuel guide for details.
