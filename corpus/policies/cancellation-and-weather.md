# Cancellation and Weather Policy

Plans change, and weather in the Kettle Basin Canoe Area doesn't always cooperate. This page explains what happens to your booking if you cancel, if you don't show up, if the area is closed, or if wind or weather changes your trip once it has started. We've tried to keep it simple and fair to everyone, including the next group waiting for the same boats.

## How to cancel

To cancel a booking, email desk@paddlesectionsupply.example or call us at (555) 555-0147 during store hours (6 a.m. to 8 p.m. daily in season). We count the days before pickup from the date we receive your email or take your call, not from the date on your reservation confirmation. If you email after hours, we'll confirm the next morning, but the date you sent it is the date that counts.

Please include your name, your pickup date and the name the reservation is under. We'll reply with a written confirmation of the cancellation and what, if anything, is being refunded or credited.

## Cancellation schedule

How much you get back depends on how far ahead of your pickup date you cancel.

| When you cancel | What you get |
|---|---|
| 30 or more days before pickup | Full refund, minus a $25 processing fee |
| 14 to 29 days before pickup | 50% refund |
| Less than 14 days before pickup | No refund, but a 100% credit valid for 12 months |
| No-show | The booking is forfeited |

### 30 days or more

Cancel 30 or more days before your pickup date and we refund everything you've paid us, minus a $25 processing fee. At that distance we usually have time to rebook the boats and gear.

### 14 to 29 days

Cancel 14 to 29 days before pickup and we refund 50% of what you've paid.

### Less than 14 days

Inside 14 days we can rarely fill the boats again, so there's no refund. Instead, we give you a credit for 100% of what you've paid. The credit is good for 12 months from the date you cancel and can be used toward any rental, outfitting package or shuttle with us. It's tied to the name on the original booking, but anyone in your original group is welcome to come with you when you use it.

### No-shows

If you don't show up for your pickup and haven't told us you're cancelling, the booking is forfeited. There's no refund and no credit. If you're running behind on pickup day, call us. A group that calls to say they'll be a few hours late is not a no-show, and we'll hold the gear for you through the end of that day.

## Permit fees

The Kettle Basin Wilderness Office charges a $6 reservation fee for each permit. That fee goes to the Wilderness Office, not to us, and it is never refunded, no matter when or why you cancel, including when the area is closed. The cancellation schedule above applies only to what you've paid Paddle Section Supply.

## Area closures

Sometimes the Kettle Basin Wilderness Office closes all or part of the area, usually for a fire or after a major storm. If the Wilderness Office closes the area before your pickup, you can choose either:

- to reschedule your trip for free, or
- a full refund, with no processing fee.

It doesn't matter how close to your pickup date the closure happens. This applies whenever the closure is announced before you pick up your gear. If you choose to reschedule, we'll work with you on new dates and help you get a new permit for them. Remember that the $6 permit reservation fee is still not refunded by the Wilderness Office.

If a closure is announced while you're already out on your trip, follow the Wilderness Office's instructions and get out safely. How a closure affects your return time is covered in the late returns section of our rental agreement.

## Wind and weather on launch day

Wind is the biggest hazard on the big lakes here. Open water like Ashby Lake can go from calm to whitecaps in less than an hour, and a loaded canoe in rough water is no place to be.

On launch day, our staff may delay your launch for wind. We do this when we believe the crossing from our dock or the drop-off at your entry point isn't safe for the boats and group we're sending out. Staff make the call at the dock, and the decision is final for that departure. Most delays last a few hours; winds often lie down in the late afternoon or early the next morning.

There are no partial-day refunds for wind delays. Your rental days are counted the same way whether you launch at 7 a.m. or 4 p.m. If a delay affects a shuttle departure, we'll put you on the next departure with room, and the shuttle service policy has the departure times.

Rain, cold and bugs on their own aren't reasons for a delay or a refund. Pack good rain gear and warm layers. The forecast is posted at the counter every morning.

## Leaving early

If you come off the water before your rental ends, whether because of weather, a change of plans, or just a tough trip, there is no refund for the unused days. We're always glad to see you back safely, and we'd much rather you come in early than push on in conditions you're not comfortable with.

## Changes that aren't cancellations

Want to move your dates, add a boat or change your gear? Call us as early as you can and we'll tell you what's available and how the change affects your booking. Keep in mind that a permit is good for one entry point and one entry date, so new dates usually mean a new permit.

## Questions

If you're not sure how this policy applies to your booking, call (555) 555-0147 or email desk@paddlesectionsupply.example. We'd rather talk it through before you cancel than after.
