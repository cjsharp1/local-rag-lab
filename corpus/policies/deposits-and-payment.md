# Deposits and Payment

This page covers how you pay for a rental with Paddle Section Supply, how much deposit we hold, and how and when you get it back. Rental prices are on our current rate sheet.

## Booking payment

To hold a reservation, we take a booking payment of $50 per boat. That payment isn't an extra charge. It's applied to your rental, so it comes off the amount you pay at pickup.

You can make the booking payment by phone at (555) 555-0147 or in person at the store. Once it's in, we'll email you a confirmation with your dates, boats and gear list.

If you cancel, what happens to your booking payment depends on how far ahead you cancel. See our cancellation and weather policy.

## Paying for your rental

The full rental is paid at pickup, before you load up. That includes boats, camping gear, outfitting packages, shuttles and anything you buy at the counter, less the booking payment you've already made.

### Payment methods we accept

- Visa
- Mastercard
- Discover
- Cash

We don't accept personal checks.

## Deposits

Every rental needs a deposit, held from pickup until your gear is checked back in. The person who signs the rental agreement provides the deposit.

| Rental | Deposit |
|---|---|
| Each boat, credit card hold | $200 per boat |
| Gear-only rental (no boat), credit card hold | $100 |
| Each boat, cash instead of a card hold | $300 per boat |

### Credit card holds

Our standard deposit is a hold on your credit card: $200 for each boat you rent, or $100 if you're renting camping gear without a boat. A hold isn't a charge. The amount is set aside on your card, so it reduces your available credit while you're out, but nothing is taken from your account unless there's a deduction.

The rental agreement requires a credit card for the deposit, so please bring the card in the signer's name to pickup, along with a government-issued photo ID.

### Cash deposits

If you'd rather leave cash than have a hold on your card, the deposit is $300 per boat. We count it with you at the counter, and you get a written receipt. Please keep that receipt with your rental agreement and bring it back when you return.

## Getting your deposit back

Your deposit is released within 5 business days of your return. For a card hold, that means we release the hold; how long your bank takes to show the full amount available again is up to your card issuer. For a cash deposit, we return it to you at check-in if there are no deductions, or mail you a check for the balance if there are.

The clock starts when your gear is checked in. Gear left at the after-hours drop is checked in the next morning.

## Deductions

If we need to make a deduction, for damage, lost gear, or a charge under the rental agreement, we'll send you an itemized list, with photos, within 3 business days of your return. The list shows each item, what we found and the amount. Damage and loss charges are set out in our damage and loss policy.

We take deductions out of the deposit, and the remainder is released on the same schedule. If the charges come to more than the deposit, we'll charge the difference to the card on file, and we'll send you the itemized list first.

If you think a deduction is wrong, call or email us within 30 days of receiving the list. We'll go through the photos with you, and if we've made a mistake we'll fix it.

## A few practical tips

- Check your card's available credit before your trip. Holds can be several hundred dollars for a group with two or three boats.
- Make sure the card you bring belongs to the person signing the agreement.
- Keep your rental agreement in a dry bag; you'll want it at check-in.
