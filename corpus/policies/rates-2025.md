## Notes

### Shuttle codes

Shuttles are listed by entry point code. The codes are the Kettle Basin Wilderness Office's entry point numbers, the same ones printed on your permit. Shuttle prices are per group and cover both the drop-off and the pickup. Departure times and booking rules are in our shuttle service policy.

### Group discount

Groups renting 3 or more boats for 5 or more days get 10% off boat rentals.

### Damage waiver

The damage waiver is optional and can only be bought at pickup. What it covers is explained in our damage and loss policy.

### Outfitting packages

Complete outfitting includes your boat, all the camping gear you need and your food for the trip. Partial outfitting includes your boat and camping gear, and you bring your own food. Both are priced per person per day.

### Fuel

The StormCell 2 stove kit comes with a 1-liter pot, a pot support and a windscreen. It burns isobutane canisters, which are sold at the counter, not rented.

### Late returns

Late returns: see the rental agreement.

### Deposits and payment

A deposit is required with every rental, and the full rental is paid at pickup. See our deposits and payment policy for amounts and accepted payment methods. Questions? Call (555) 555-0147 or email desk@paddlesectionsupply.example.
