# Rental Agreement

This is the agreement you sign when you pick up a canoe, kayak or camping gear from Paddle Section Supply. We've written it in plain language so you know exactly what you're agreeing to before you get to the counter. If anything here is unclear, call us at (555) 555-0147 or email desk@paddlesectionsupply.example and we'll walk you through it.

Paddle Section Supply, 14 Dock Road, Ashby Landing.

## Who can rent

- The person signing the agreement must be at least 18 years old.
- You must show a government-issued photo ID at pickup.
- You must give us a credit card for the deposit. (Details on amounts and how holds work are in our deposits and payment policy.)

The person who signs is responsible for all the gear on the agreement, even if other members of the group are the ones paddling or carrying it. If your group is splitting into two separate rentals, each one needs its own signer.

## What's included with every boat

Every boat rental comes with:

- Paddles: two per canoe, and one per kayak seat
- One PFD (life jacket) per person in your group
- Yoke pads for portaging
- A 50-ft rope

Tell us how many people are in your group and their sizes when you book, so we can have the right PFDs ready at pickup.

## Pickup and return times

- **Pickup:** from 7 a.m. on the first day of your rental.
- **Return:** all gear is due back by 6 p.m. on the last day of your rental.

A rental day runs from pickup to the 6 p.m. return deadline, so a three-day rental picked up Friday morning is due back Sunday by 6 p.m. Our store is open 6 a.m. to 8 p.m. daily during the season, and the extra hours after the deadline give us time to check your gear in properly.

When you return, bring everything to the dock or the counter and a staff member will check it in with you. Please unpack your gear, empty food barrels and canisters, and leave tents unrolled if they're wet so we can inspect and dry them.

## Late returns and the late fee

Gear returned after 6 p.m. on the due date is charged a late fee of $35 per boat for each day or part of a day it is late. It's charged per boat, not per person and not per item of camping gear, so a group with two canoes that comes in the next morning pays it twice.

We set the deadline at 6 p.m. for a practical reason. Most of our boats go straight back out the next morning, often with a group that has a permit for that entry date and can't wait. Every evening our staff inspect, clean and repair the day's returns, and a boat that arrives after the deadline usually can't be ready for a 7 a.m. pickup. When one group runs over, another group is often the one that feels it.

If you know you're running behind, let us know as early as you can. There is no cell service past Ashby Lake, so your phone won't help you once you're out on the water. If you rented a satellite messenger from us, you can send a text to the store from anywhere in the Kettle Basin Canoe Area; the number is saved in the device and printed on the card in its case. If you didn't bring one, call us as soon as you reach a spot with service, or ask another party heading out to pass a message along. A heads-up doesn't cancel the charge, but it does tell us you're safe, lets us rearrange the next morning's boats, and keeps us from starting to worry about an overdue group.

Please don't take risks to beat the deadline. Crossing big open water in rising wind, paddling after dark, or rushing a long portage to save a few dollars is never worth it. We would much rather see you the next morning than hear that you tried to make it across Ashby Lake in whitecaps.

For groups that get back after the store has closed for the day, we have an after-hours return drop next to the dock. It has a boat rack for canoes and kayaks and a key box for your rental agreement and any small items like satellite messengers and water filters. The drop is open until 10 p.m. Rack your boats hull-up, lock them with the cable provided, put your agreement in an envelope from the box with your name and the time you returned, and drop it in the slot. Stack the rest of your gear under the covered shelf beside the rack. We check everything in first thing the next morning and will call you if anything is missing or damaged. Keep in mind that the after-hours drop is a convenience, not an extension: gear dropped there after 6 p.m. still counts as late.

There is one situation where we don't apply this fee. If the Kettle Basin Wilderness Office closes the area, or issues a wind advisory that keeps your group off the water on its exit day, the charge is waived. Wilderness Office advisories are numbered, and the number is posted at entry point kiosks and announced on the weather radio channel. Write it down, and give the advisory number to our staff when you return so we can match it to your exit date and remove the charge from your bill.

## Care of equipment

You're responsible for using the gear the way it was meant to be used and bringing it back in the condition you got it, apart from normal wear like scratches and scuffs.

- Never drag a canoe over rocks or the landing; lift it clear, even for a few feet.
- Load and unload boats while they're floating, not while they're resting on the shore.
- Tie boats up or carry them well above the waterline at night and whenever you leave them, even for a short time.
- Don't stand in a canoe, and don't use paddles to push off from rocks.
- Keep stoves and fuel away from tents, and never cook inside a tent.
- Dry tents and sleeping bags whenever you get the chance on a multi-day trip.

If something breaks on your trip, do what you need to do to get home safely, then tell us when you return. Don't try to make permanent repairs yourself. Charges for damage and lost gear are listed in our damage and loss policy, along with the optional damage waiver you can buy at pickup.

## Where the gear can go

- Rented gear may not leave the Kettle Basin Canoe Area. Our boats are for trips in the area only, not for loading onto your car and taking elsewhere.
- Rented gear may not be towed behind a motorboat. If you're using our shuttle, the boats ride on the shuttle, not behind anyone else's boat.

Shuttle times and how to reserve them are in our shuttle service policy.

## Permits and regulations

The Kettle Basin Wilderness Office issues permits and sets the regulations for the area. As a permit-issuing cooperator, we can issue your permit at pickup, but following the rules on the water is up to you and your group. Fines and removal from the area are between you and the Wilderness Office, and they don't change anything in this agreement.
