# Shuttle Service

Many of the best trips in the Kettle Basin Canoe Area start somewhere other than our dock on Ashby Lake. Some entry points can only be reached by water, and some routes start in one place and finish in another. Our shuttles get you, your boats and your gear to the start of your trip and bring you home at the end of it.

For prices, see the current rate sheet.

## Two kinds of shuttle

### Tow boat

Our tow boats carry your group, your canoes or kayaks and your gear across the water to entry points that have no road access, or where water is the easier way in. Your boats ride on racks on the tow boat; they are never towed in the water behind it.

We run tow boats to:

- **Heron Lake**, which has no road. The tow boat is the only way to reach the Heron Lake landing. The trip takes about 50 minutes from our dock.
- **Raven Bay**, which can also be reached by a rough road, but most groups prefer to skip the drive.

### Van

Our van carries your group with your boats on a trailer to entry points reached by road, and picks you up at the end of your trip. We run vans to:

- **Fern Creek**, for groups who would rather not drive the gravel road themselves.
- **Upper Granite River**, with pickup at the **Granite River Take-out**. This is a one-way trip: we drop you at the top of the river and meet you at the take-out at the end.
- **Stillwater Lake**, with pickup at **Kettle Lake**. Also one-way: we drop you at the Stillwater Lake put-in and pick you up at Kettle Lake.

Every shuttle price on the rate sheet covers both the drop-off and the pickup, and is charged per group.

## Departure times

All shuttles leave from our dock at 14 Dock Road, Ashby Landing.

| Shuttle | Departures from the dock |
|---|---|
| Tow boat | 7 a.m., 10 a.m. and 1 p.m. |
| Van | 8 a.m. and noon |

Please be at the dock with your gear packed and your permit in hand at least 30 minutes before your departure. Shuttles leave on time so the rest of the day's schedule doesn't slip, and we can't hold a departure for one group.

If staff delay a launch for wind, we'll move you to the next departure with room. See our cancellation and weather policy for how wind delays work.

## Reserving a shuttle

Reserve your shuttle at least 48 hours ahead. You can reserve it when you book your rental, or later by phone at (555) 555-0147 or by email at desk@paddlesectionsupply.example. Space on each departure is limited, and the early tow boats fill first in July and August, so book early if you have a preferred time.

When you reserve, tell us:

- Your entry point and entry date (they need to match your permit)
- How many people and how many boats are in your group
- Your pickup point and the date and time you plan to be there

## Your permit and the shuttle

A permit is good for one entry point and one entry date, and your group has to enter on that date at that entry point. Book your shuttle for the same entry point and date as your permit. If you're heading to Heron Lake, your permit must be for the Heron Lake entry point. We can't drop you at a different entry point from the one on your permit.

## Pickups

For tow boat trips, we set your pickup date and time when you reserve. Be at the landing at the agreed time with your gear ready to load.

For van trips, the driver meets you at the take-out on the date and time we agreed when you reserved. If you're running behind, there's no cell service past Ashby Lake, so a satellite messenger is the best way to reach us. If you're early, wait at the landing; please don't try to walk out on the road.

Your rental's return deadline still applies when you use a shuttle, so plan your pickup with time to get back to the dock. See the rental agreement for return times.

## What to bring and what to leave

- Pack everything in packs or barrels. Loose items get lost in transit.
- Fuel travels sealed in the tow boat's fuel locker. Hand your fuel to the operator when you board, and collect it at the landing.
- Dogs are welcome on the shuttle if they're leashed.
- Wear your PFD on the tow boat.

## Parking

If you're driving to us and taking a shuttle, you can leave your car in our lot while you're out. Parking prices are on the rate sheet.
